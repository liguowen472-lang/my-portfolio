关键设计：把文本拆成两个版本。给用户看的强调可读性；给下游生成用的只做必要纠错，避免文学加工让画面偏离原始梦境。Key decision: split the text into two versions — readable for users, minimally corrected for generation, so images and video stay true to the original dream.

问题 → 动作Problem → Action

团队原以为瓶颈在生成和付费环节。我独立编写埋点文档，在 GA4 搭建转化漏斗，发现主要流失其实在第一步「梦境记录」。The team assumed the bottleneck was generation or paywall. I wrote the tracking spec and built a GA4 funnel — the real drop-off was at step one: recording the dream.

用户访谈显示，人们多在刚醒、很困时记录，打字成本高。我推动把语音设为默认入口、文字作为备选。Interviews showed people record right after waking, when typing is a chore. I pushed to make voice the default input, with text as fallback.

「懂王锐评」“Sharp Review” feature

基于用户调研和 ASR 文本洞察，独立优化并落地 Prompt，让总结不止复述内容，而是给出有观点的判断。「懂王锐评」成为最受好评的功能。Using user research and patterns in ASR transcripts, I rewrote the prompts so summaries take a point of view instead of just restating. “Sharp Review” became the best-received feature.

商业模式Monetisation

转写与大模型调用按量计费，用得越多成本越高。我拆解竞品订阅模式，设计三层积分：免费体验、会员月度积分、按需加购——用积分统一不同功能的成本，也便于后续调价。Transcription and LLM calls scale with usage. After unpacking competitors’ subscriptions, I designed a three-tier credit system — free trial, monthly member credits, top-ups — unifying feature costs and keeping pricing flexible.
